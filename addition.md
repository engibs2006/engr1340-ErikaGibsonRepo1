When adding two integers with the same sign add their absolute values (the distance of a number from zero, always positive) and keep the original sign.
Example: 2+2=4, |2|+|2|=4
Example: -3+-3=-6, |-3|+|-3|=3+3=6 (keep original sign)----> -6

an example of how two integer multiplication work would be: 2 x 3 = 6
